03 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE GARDEN

Hi, I’m Alicia.

I’m drawn to people who care deeply about what they’re building, and to the unexpected connections that help them move forward.

My work is about helping early-stage companies access the talent, customers, partners, advisors, and investors they need to grow. I like understanding what someone is really trying to do, then thinking about who might help.

Outside that, I’m curious about AI, personal growth, and how we make a meaningful life. I think in connections, often out loud. A good conversation is one of my favorite places to begin.
