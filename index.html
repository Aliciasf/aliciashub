01 / THE HUMAN LAYER

The future is intelligent.
Let’s make it human.

More possibility begins with seeing each other more clearly.

In a world shaped by AI, I’m interested in what makes us uniquely us: our talents, our intuition, the things we care enough to build.

I connect people whose talents, passions, and purpose can move something forward. A person, a team, a company, a technology. I care about creating value on every side of the connection, and helping good ideas find the people who can bring them into the world.

04 / HI, I’M ALICIA

A people person.
A possibility person.

I’m drawn to the spark that makes someone unmistakably themselves. The talent they light up talking about. The idea they can’t leave alone. The thing they’re here to contribute.

My work is helping early-stage companies connect with the talent, customers, partners, advisors, and investors they need to grow. I care about the human fit as much as the practical one.

I think in connections, often out loud. I’m happiest in a real conversation, following a thread with someone curious, and discovering where our worlds might meet.

Curiosity. Intuition. Intentional action.Alicia

A PERSONAL PHILOSOPHY

A little synchronicity.
A little intention.

I’m drawn to Carl Jung’s idea of synchronicity: meaningful coincidences. The unexpected conversation. The person you meet just as a new question is taking shape.

I believe a meaningful encounter can change our trajectory. My part is to notice the possibility, make a thoughtful connection, and help it become something real.
